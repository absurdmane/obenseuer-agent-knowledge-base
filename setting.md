## The game

- Developer/publisher: Loiste Interactive (Finland), makers of INFRA. Same city as INFRA.
- Urban survival RPG + landlord/life sim, Early Access. Tone: bleak, gritty, socially critical, darkly comic.
  Official tagline: "the only way is up!"
- Premise: through a clerical error you get an unfinished, run-down tenement in Obenseuer. You fix it up, rent it out,
  farm, brew, manufacture, scavenge, trade and do crime. Needs, addictions, depression and a fungal disease are simulated.
- Setting mood: cold wet November, crumbling concrete, a Finland-like country (Stolland).

## The world

- **Stalburg** — the city. **Obenseuer** — its worst district, nicknamed **"Open Sewer"** (after mining slag leaked into the canals).
- Former mining town. Mine closed, steel works went bankrupt → mass unemployment. Poor-quality concrete housing,
  ignored by a corrupt planning department.
- The city dumps homeless people and "the rabble" here. The district is under **quarantine**: nobody leaves.
  You need an Obenseuer passport to move between zones.
- **Stalburg Mushroom Virus (SMV)** — fungal disease, spread by spores; mushrooms are also a drug.
- Alcoholism is everywhere (a local brewery, **Osmo Olut**). Home-brewed **kilju** is illegal in-universe.
- Money: **Open Sewer Coins (OC)**, nearly worthless, vs **RM ("Real Money")**. Welfare is paid in coins.
- Zones: Residential (start, the player's tenement), Bazaar, Mines, Swamp, Central, Quarantine, Turnip Hill
  (a homeless self-governed housing estate).
- Powers that be: the **Obenseuer Militia** (checkpoints, jail), the Overlords (Residential), the Leaders of the Bazaar,
  the Turnip Hill Administration, the bureaucracy.
- Running jokes: the "Open Sewer" pun, rubber ducks ("Quack!"), turnips as the staple crop and currency of life,
  trash food, no health-and-safety anywhere.

## Useful themes for mod names

Bureaucracy and paperwork, welfare and benefits, quarantine, turnips, the militia, coins vs real money, ducks,
the crumbling tenement, the clerical error.
