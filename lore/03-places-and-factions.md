# Obenseuer: Places and Factions

Scope: the tenement blocks and their floors, the Bazaar and its districts, the mines and their sub-locations, the Bergmann tunnels and the Bank of Bazaar, the metro, the city gate and the Wasteland, the outside areas, and every faction, company and authority the sources name, with how they relate. Characters are in 02-characters.md; quests are in 04-questlines.md; the world's rules and history are in 05-world-and-lore.md.

## 1. Map in brief

Obenseuer is one district of the city of Stalburg, in Stolland. It is made of several connected parts:

- **Tenement blocks** (the Open Sewer proper): the Center doorphone building, Deekula A, B and C, Tenement A, Tenement B, Kolhola, the Tenement market and the O-Market, the One Stop Shop, the speakeasy and the Kurahaara farm.
- **The Bazaar**: a gated enclave with a gatehouse, a Chamber of Commerce, a jail, a casino, a bank, a sauna, a bookshop, a co-op, a mall, a slum, and the Murju block. An industrial zone and an elevator are controlled from it.
- **The mines**: old ore workings under the district, reached from a service area with elevators, crushers and a canteen. The mines hold claims, a potato farm, a hotel, a repository and the Bergmann tunnels to the Bazaar bank.
- **The metro**: old tunnels built for a national programme, now used for smuggling.
- **The gate and the Wasteland**: a keypad gate with a system voice (G.A.L.E.N), and a wasteland outside the city limit with ghosts and red rocks.
- **Outside**: Black Swamp, the Stalburg Central district, Orefell and other towns, the quarantine zones across the canal and planned on Whiprock island and in Uhlu.

Many addresses in the sources are formatted as Obenseuer Road 2 with a building letter and apartment number, with the postcode 05500 Stalburg.

## 2. The tenement blocks

### 2.1 The player's building and the Center doorphone building

The player is given a building that the texts never name. Several characters refer to it: Malone's old tenement, the building by the canal (recognised by Lasse), the building over the unstable mine tunnel (Toivo), and the building where the Crazy Neighbor has an apartment. The sources treat these as probably one building, but this is not stated.

The building is unfinished and poorly maintained. Its floors, stairwells and apartments are renovated in stages. Its features in the texts:

- **Apartments** that can be renovated and rented to tenants. The renovation levels unlock contractor work and janitor tasks (renovation 3 unlocks firewood, 4 unlocks septic tanks, per Malone's chain).
- **Upgrades**: balcony, facade, courtyard, apartment walls. The Chamber of Commerce wants facade level 3, courtyard level 2 and walls level 1 before it opens its shop next door [453].
- **Janitor services** by Lizzy Mopper [213]: fill the water tanks (435 OC), empty septic tanks (620 OC), bring firewood.
- **A resources chest** where the player can drop collected material.
- **Stairwell and elevator**: the building has an elevator-related subplot through Jonne Reis [189].
- **Unstable tunnel** below the building, which Toivo says will collapse (see the mines section).

The Center doorphone building [66] is a separate five-floor building with a doorphone. Its floors are:

- **Floor 1**: Berg, Hietala, Abrahamsson, Halko.
- **Floor 2**: Eskola, Walker, Adams, Huang.
- **Floor 3**: Samuelsson, Falk, Moilanen, Ilves.
- **Floor 4**: Skuja, Jansons, Rummo, Putilin.
- **Floor 5**: Grachyov, Kettunen, Taylor, Ylitalo.

Most doors buzz or swear. It is quest-independent. The surnames overlap with Tenement A (Hietala, Eskola, Taylor), so it may be a sister block of Tenement A (unclear).

### 2.2 Deekula (A, B, C)

Deekula is a complex of blocks with numbered flats. Named flats and their residents:

- **Deekula A**: a storage area with a sign ([COPYRIGHT]), a storage with a dying writer's note about a bad product, and the Deekula A8 note.
- **Deekula B**: the mines access is in the basement of Deekula B (door password [COPYRIGHT], per Eliana). Hanss and Anya meet here. Flat B1 is the priest's (Father Tobias's), with a hidden access behind a bookshelf. Flat B3 is Amanda Korhonen's, with a painter neighbour, a bathroom body and a woman in bed. Storage B3 and storage B4 connect (storage B4 is Amanda's). Flat B9 is the subject of a relocation note (residents to be moved within one day). Flat B10 is on the top floor, where Max Masher lives behind the "evil door". A rooftop is suggested as the launch point for Jane Miller's drone. Several bodies were found in B3 and in the storage (per police notes).
- **Deekula C**: the top floor is the base of the Svamppatrullen gang (Ville's rival). Hank Möbelmann's furniture shop is here. Pia's makeshift two-story house with a balcony is next to Deekula C, with a storage opposite the front door (keys with Alice). Dr. Pena's clinic is in Deekula. Apartment C6 is David Lund's. The Deekula C storage is where a body was found.
- **Deekula north gate**: David Lund's toll bridge is behind the building, near the north gate.

Deekula has its own Max's door, a bridge and a toll, a clinic, a furniture shop, a mash flat, a gang base, a priest, a neighbour who is a painter, and a wasteland approach. The police describe several fungal deaths in Deekula B.

### 2.3 Tenement A

Tenement A is the cluster that mixes Fredrik's lab, the Hietala flat and a lantern factory:

- **Apartment A1**: a body was found by the police report (the AliceShack location).
- **Apartment A10**: Fredrik's flat, with an unofficial lab, an operating room and a computer room. The doorphone opens only after Anya's KnowsFredrik flag is set; Louie Green asks for a name before he opens.
- **Second floor**: Joel Hietala's flat. Johannes Eskola lives nearby. A stairwell where Alice was seen with Joel and a woman.
- **Lantern factory**: run by Louie Green and his wife Ayla. Hires workers at 65 OC per hour for an eight-hour shift. Makes mushroom lanterns, which are part of the local economy.
- **Elevator**: out of service, with an electrician called. A fuse board with lights, engine, controls and call buttons (a note asks for help from the top floor).
- **Duck vending machine** upstairs, darts, and a keycard "to Tenement A".
- **Emma-Maija Taylor's cat trade** and Jonne Reis's elevator work.

### 2.4 Tenement B

Tenement B is the building behind the O-Market, reached down an alley. Passmore lives here, with a private bridge from his flat to the Bazaar building that he uses to move stock past the border. A second Passmore may live in the same building (unclear). The Bergmann tunnel entrance and its keypad ([COPYRIGHT]) are in the building or nearby. A sleeping spot by the entrance is mentioned.

### 2.5 Kolhola

Kolhola is a residential building with a resident committee, and the home of Eliana's photography shop on the right side of the Bazaar gate, Julius Öberg's mushroom door, Meri Haka, Börje Nyberg, Liouba Bennett, Rauha Taisto, Jörge Af Guldsköld's office, Sam Morton and the Warehom Hostel's pods.

The **Kolhola rules** [12145180852264] are a resident committee's list of house rules. They include: no noise from 18:00 to 06:00; no loitering; no smoking; no possession, consumption or growing of green mushrooms or products made from them (violators are evicted at once); alcohol only in reserved sauna turns or from 18:00 to 21:00 on Fridays and Saturdays, at most two bottles of beer; no alcohol production; no urinating or defecating outside toilets.

Kolhola's residential A-codes (A2, A6, A17) are notes with number codes. What they open is unclear.

The Kolhola building also contains the telemarketing office for the OS magazine ([COPYRIGHT]) and the Warehom Hostel [80] (see below).

### 2.6 The Tenement market, the O-Market and the One Stop Shop

- **The Tenement market** is a row of stalls (food, spirits, canned goods, firewood, plants, hardware, souvenirs and a janitor service). Details are in 02-characters.md, section 3.
- **The O-Market** is always open. Its roof has a farm (a big sign). It has a bottle-return machine, an Evil Bear candy bag seller, building-material stalls, Jomppe's seed stall, a mailbox, and a shop where Mirjam Freighter sells cigarettes and scratchcards. The stairs down to the bell and buzzer of Pentti's speakeasy are near it. Passmore's Tenement B is behind it.
- **The One Stop Shop** is a robot-run shop next to the player's tenement that sells all kinds of things. Its clerk is described as strange. Mr. Duck's blue duck was last seen in the building near it.

### 2.7 The speakeasy

Pentti Penttilä's speakeasy is in a Tenement building, behind a door with an intercom (Mr. Door Phone). The door opens for someone who claims to be Malone's assistant. The bar has a secret distillery room behind a button on the bar shelf. Mr. Duck lives there. Simppa Kurahaara works the bar. The speakeasy is reached through the O-Market's stairs.

### 2.8 Redemption

Redemption is a church and charity building in the Bazaar gatehouse area, run by Fidan Tanner. It serves pea soup outside daily, and prayers come before the soup. Donors and volunteers get the Bazaar door key once they have a passport and a Bazaar home. Redemption also has a hole in the wall of the Gatehouse area where Colton lives (the sources place the hole in the Gatehouse and the Redemption building, unclear).

### 2.9 The Osmo Hill bar

A bar where Keijo Korsonen and "Mark" once drank. Named only in passing.

## 3. The Bazaar

The Bazaar is a gated enclave with its own gatehouse, its own bank, its own jail and its own rules. Entry needs a passport. Living there needs a Bazaar house and proof of residence. Most of the Bazaar's districts are named in the sources, and they are described here as the player meets them.

### 3.1 The gatehouse

The gatehouse is the border checkpoint. Axel Norberg checks passports and bags, sells the registration form at 100 RM, and can be bribed. Refusing a bag check sends the player to prison for a short sentence. Forged passports pass the gate (the sources note the forged passport items are recognised and accepted). Outsiders are sometimes used as a negotiating tool by the Bazaar in its quarrels with the government.

Past the gatehouse are the Redemption building, the Mentti district and the Bazaar's roads, which lead to the Chamber, the mall and the jail.

### 3.2 N-O Company and the Chamber of Commerce

The Bazaar HQ is called N-O Company. It is a tall wooden building with a round stone tower at the far end of the street. It registers people for elevator and mine access, and reviews each person. Karl Hagen, the Bazaar's leader ([COPYRIGHT]), works here. The Chamber of Commerce occupies a separate office with a doorphone (its access is at the main gatehouse, routed past the Clocktower, per [331]). The Chamber has a conference room, a board room and a reception. Its members are Jussi Pöhinä (chairman), Vanja Bjorkman, Katriina Vermund (receptionist) and Svein Regnell. The Chamber wants to grow the Bazaar, expel the slums, and absorb the player's tenement.

### 3.3 Bazaar Industrial

A former parking garage and technical spaces, now a blast furnace and converter. Upper floors have workshops. The building sits over an old SOMC mine shaft and a ventilation shaft. Industrial zone and the surface elevator are controlled by the Bazaar. Pertti Korhonen, a maintenance man, offers smuggling into the industrial zone and the quarantine.

### 3.4 The mall (Mallment)

The Mallment is a converted apartment block with shops on several floors: a cafe, a fish market, a fruit stall and berries (second floor); a tax-free shop and Aziz's Kebab and Burger (top floor). Traders include Olle Skatt (cigarettes), Christian Sykkelsen (bikes), Marja Noukkinen (berries), Ragnar Stortorsk (fish), Eva Smörbulle (coffee and buns), Justyna Krystiana (furniture for businesses), Anni Kiinteistö (Open Homes Company realtor), Johanna Faust (OS Power reception) and Maximo Utedass (bathroom attendant). The Open Homes Company office is on the third floor of the "Mallment", probably this block.

### 3.5 The jail

The Bazaar jail has a security lobby with lockers for prisoners' belongings, cells, and off-limits areas that send a visitor to prison. It is next to the bank and the metro station. The warden sets a daily routine: wake up, breakfast, cells closed in 15 minutes, evening recreation. Riina Pamppumäki runs the lobby and refuses pizza deliveries to inmates.

### 3.6 The casino

The casino has an admin wing behind a security door, Casper's office with a desk computer, a security room, Raimo's room, a bar run by Gérard Dujardin, and a cashier, Zara Nkosi. It was owned by Casper Colikke, then taken by the Crazy Neighbor (state "Crazy"), then by Heikki Mailanen. Gambling is common across the Bazaar (card games, Ace of Spades tickets at kiosks). A tourist, Gary Lind, warns visitors about the casino.

### 3.7 Bazaar sauna and spa

The sauna and spa have pools, saunas, separate men's and women's changing rooms, and a 20 RM entry fee run by Sandra Klapi. Timo Löyly runs the sauna. Matti Kiehinen is the sauna champion and Raimo Stalreg's locker-blocker. The sauna contest can end in a death, after which the spa closes and staff write a report.

### 3.8 The Welho bookshop and lair

The bookshop is run by Aune Korsborg. Below it is Welhö's lair, a cellar reached by a keypad (the passcode is [COPYRIGHT] in the Welhö text) and by the Sacred Welhö seal. The lair is full of green mushrooms (per Aune). The bookshop may know the book [COPYRIGHT]. The Bazaar library is mentioned in the tasks as the place to ask about it.

### 3.9 Pontikka (the Moonshining Co-op) and the Garden Bar

The co-op occupies the top three floors of a building with brewing and distilling facilities. The Garden Bar is on the ground floor, where a mushroom vendor works downstairs. Courses and memberships run from an office reached by a doorphone in the lobby. Arvo Kirkastaja runs the co-op's administration, and Aune Kirkastaja runs the Garden Bar. Keke Roinaneva's shop is here (she stole the shop sign from the O-Market). The building has a big red sign.

### 3.10 The Murju block

Murju B has a locked front door with an intercom (doorphone number 25 leads to Erto's door). Erto Lagerfeld lives on the top floor. Murju also has pig pens and storage cages (Alfred Schweinstein evicted the cage tenants), and Urpo Murhapuro's flat (244). Lykke Tillbehör's cold room, Dave Smiker's "fire" shop, and Miina-Ansa Virtanen's tripwire flat are here too.

### 3.11 The slums

The slums are poor, smelly and the Bazaar's back streets. Their gates close at night. Named places in the slums:

- A **rat arena** run by Rosario Rootto, where players bet OC on rats.
- A **hut with a hatch** that sells bad pizza.
- **Mikael's hut**, with a makeshift outdoor shower by the front door.
- **Bror Stjäla's patch** (a robber's territory).
- **Kauko Poronen's TV room**, where the news is angry.
- **Rob-in Build and Repair**, in a yellow-windowed place (per Urho).
- The homes of Eetu Rymy and Kalevi Harvakattu.

The Bazaar's relocated families live in tents or outside the slum gates (Toini Karhu's roof).

### 3.12 The mall's other shops

- **Fungi Boutique** (Jasper Sporefield): mushroom products under special permission, certified mycelium. Bazaar security confines troublemakers to its area.
- **Rocket Ramen** (Minh Thienlong).
- **Scatola di Maccheroni** (Giacobbe and Dolores Bolognese): a restaurant with home delivery and reservations. A sabotage target in Akim's errand.
- **Bar Bizarre**, next to the meat shop and the casino.
- **Club Bassement**: a ticket-only club.
- **Muscle Max's 24-hour gym**: a boxing ring downstairs.
- **Cinema** (Denys Moskalenko): a single ticket covers the day's screenings.
- **Internet cafe and gaming rooms** (Tero Verkko): rooms by the night.
- **Electronics shop** (Jonas Elström): retro tech.
- **Remontti-Repe**: a hardware shop.
- **Ankea**: a shop in the Bazaar with an ad for "money-pattern" curtains.
- **Gold and jewellery shop** (Torsten Dvergsson) and an alcohol shop (Astrid Vinsjö).
- **Bread stall** (Gustav Sågspånberg) and **second-hand shop** (Sofija Pisula).
- **Osmo Olut Factory** (its gate is staffed by Topias Iisalmi): the biggest employer in Obenseuer, per the guard.
- **Sewerview Inn** (Omari Solheim): built beside the old canal.
- **Hotel Korso**: straight ahead from the mall. A Fungal Disease Treatment Unit decontaminates it, and only hotel staff and guests may enter. Heikki and the bank manager's "honeymoon" took place here, and a reservation letter from it is among the notes.
- **Mentti** (Penni-Pena's loan office) and **Mentti B** (where Heikki hides; the loan agent is here).

### 3.13 The Bank of Bazaar

The Bank of Bazaar is not an official bank. It is reached from the Bergmann water tunnels through a manhole and a drain cover, and is open to the water tunnel from 23:00 to 03:00. Its vault is opened by a code set to "[COPYRIGHT]" (per Heikki's notes). The bank's upstairs boss's room has cameras that can be disabled. Heikki robbed the vault with the manager's help. A separate Stalburg Bank (customer service by phone) exists, with branches in Orefell. It is a different institution.

### 3.14 The Bazaar's outside

The Bazaar's outside holds the Central district (which needs a permit, an ID pass and a negative test), the Stalburg Central Railway Station, the Walter Square cafe, and the Hansa Coast. The Bazaar's visitors and tourists are listed in 02-characters.md.

## 4. The mines

Obenseuer's old ore mine ran under half the district until it closed. The sources give different closing dates (see 06-open-questions.md). Mining and its permits are run by the Management office, the Bazaar (for the surface elevator) and the claim owners.

### 4.1 The service area

The service area has the elevators (including the big elevator to the surface), the ore drop-off point beside them, the minitrain crushers, a small office near the surface elevator (Reijo's ore buyer), the canteen run by Anna Markkanen, a break room (which may hold keys), and a tenement access tunnel that needs keys. It is completely collapsed at one point, and Ken says the route to the work runs through Erikson's claim.

### 4.2 The Management office

The Management office sits next to the ore buyer, and a sign on its door says "Management". Tanja Nypan works here: she gives out mining permit forms, takes the form when printed upstairs, runs the age check and the passport check, and collects the 200 RM miner's tax. A printer is upstairs. Tanja says the founders of the mining operation set the rules. She also handles the RepositoryAccessForm.

### 4.3 The ore buyer

Reijo "Reiska" Rautakaivos buys ore from miners who have a permit. He can be reached at a small office near the elevator. He is a former miner who did not go back to the mines because of a heart condition. He empties minitrains into the crushers.

### 4.4 The Canteen

Pay-first rule: take your food, then pay at the counter. Anna Markkanen runs it. Its cat, Kisu, is a plot point. The canteen has a rat problem, and a "blonde woman" who worked here (Ines) left earlier.

### 4.5 The Pickaxe Hotel

Joran Kallio's hotel has six rooms, two in permanent use. Room two has space for 18 people in shared sleeping. Two toilets are downstairs and a shower upstairs. Joran misses the sea.

### 4.6 The Sauna Club

An all-male club that meets in a mine space. Laura Markkanen uses a space there when the members are away. Einar Kaivuri's crew and Seamus Liepa have been here. A crew apartment exists on the surface.

### 4.7 The monitoring room

Mikkel Purola's room watches the speed cameras along the minitrain track and runs the minitrain collection. Minitrains of ore are emptied into the crushers. Mikkel fines speeding and can be bribed.

### 4.8 The claims

Claims are ore veins worked with tipping wagons and minitrains.

- **Antero Erikson's claim** (the claim the player can work): the player mines ore, hauls it to the service area and takes 20 percent of the receipt. Antero lost the keys to the other gate.
- **The Kauha claim** (Emil Kauha): the player mines ore for 20 percent of the receipt value. The Kauha claim has an electrical building that needs keys, one of which a woman took and one of which Pertti lost. Ines tried to get into a building on this claim, looking for something.
- **Ivar's place**: front door, a vending machine (shrooms and orange powder), and a restricted cave.
- **The potato mine**: Seamus's underground farm, which sells the cheapest potatoes in Obenseuer. It is reached by a route found through the mines.

### 4.9 The fuel pump

Anders Terva fills minitrains on credit, paid later in OC. Warns not to drink the diesel.

### 4.10 The underground lake and the island

The underground lake is deep and cold. A crazy man lives near it, and Seamus's silver ring is said to be with him. The island in the lake belongs to Saunaklonkku, a mine creature that accepts fish and silver, and refuses gold.

### 4.11 The blue mushroom cave

Ivar stumbled into a cave of blue mushrooms and odd plants. He felt dizzy, the cave seemed to spin, and he woke outside. He calls the place cursed.

### 4.12 The western tunnels and the repository

The western tunnels hold an old radioactive waste repository. Entry needs a RepositoryAccessForm (from Tanja) and a dosimeter. The repository gate is opened with the code [COPYRIGHT] (given by Heska). Heska guards it from a small room deep in the old mine.

The **dumping tunnel** holds spent fuel casks. One cask fell and broke open, making the tunnel deadly radioactive. A blast door at its end is wired to radiation meters and does not open when radiation is above 50 millisieverts per hour. Minutes inside mean a fatal dose, and it gives no sign.

Behind the repository is another side where someone lives, reached by a route that has collapsed (per Heska). A Bazaar team broke into the western tunnels and found the radioactive deposit (per Ivar).

### 4.13 The collapsed tunnel under the player's tenement

A mine tunnel under the player's building is unstable. Ken says it must be cleared and reinforced before his team can work; Toivo says the building will collapse without repairs. The clearing path runs through Erikson's claim. A different collapsed tunnel, on the route to Heska's repository gate, can be cleared by the player, who is rewarded with an item (per Heska).

### 4.14 OS Mining Services

Toivo Malmijoki's company. It sells machinery, builds tunnel supports, conveyors and frameworks, and has a sales floor in the same building as Ken's team. Ivar calls it [COPYRIGHT].

### 4.15 The surface elevator

The big elevator to the surface needs Bazaar permission, handled in a building next to it. Reijo says mentioning that he sent the player might help. Tor Bergstahl issues elevator access after a registration check and a Mining Permit.

## 5. The Bergmann tunnels and the Bank of Bazaar

The Bergmann water tunnels run under the Bazaar and reach the Bank of Bazaar through a manhole. They are reached from a door with a keypad ([COPYRIGHT]) near the building entrance in Tenement B. Their state is described in several notes.

- The tunnels are renovated except for the parts near Obenseuer, and get worse every day (note by Mike, the new worker).
- The former worker, Robin, changed the flood door settings after the flood sensors stopped working. The fix is to close the floodgates linked to that tunnel and open main 02.
- The **control room fuses** are labelled floodgate control A1, floodgate control A2, microwave and B3.
- The **flood door sign** says a red light means the area may be flooded or the door has a fault and cannot be opened; green means safe, and the door opens only on green. Do not stand under the door while it closes.
- **Storage B3** (Robin): Robin heard breathing, thought the place was cursed, bricked up the door and took the flood door's handwheel. He refers to something that happened in a tunnel near Hammer Valley.
- **Tunnel B2**: a note says that something (a mannequin-like object) moves on its own, and that the hallway is shorter than it used to be. A door password, possibly [COPYRIGHT]. The tunnel has a monster called a mannequin monster in one task.
- **Another tunnel B2** is at Hammer Valley, far away (per Anya).
- The bank's drain cover is open from 23:00 to 03:00. The vault is [COPYRIGHT]. The boss's room upstairs has the cameras.

Mike's body in the Bergmann tunnels had a key labelled B2 (per tasks). The key opens tunnel B2.

## 6. The metro

The metro runs under Obenseuer, built in old tunnels that became the Stolland Nuclear programme's route (S.N.W) in the 1970s. The notes also name an East Port metro line; the Bazaar jail sits beside a metro station. The sources give the first line's opening as 1953 in one answer and 1962 in another (see open questions).

- **S.N.W's bunker**: a metro train ended up in a bunker after a signal error in 1986. Mason says the surviving passengers were executed to keep the programme secret, and that the programme was discontinued afterwards. Mason is unsure of details.
- **Smuggling**: an Obenseuer gang used the tunnels to smuggle mushrooms and moonshine.
- **Castle Rock Island**: an island with a drawbridge to the mainland. Mason first called it Steinburg Island and corrected himself.

## 7. The city gate and the Wasteland

### 7.1 The city gate

A keypad gate at the edge of the district. The digits are read out as [COPYRIGHT] ([COPYRIGHT]). It opens when the CityGateOpen flag is set. The gate system is a voice that warns an entity not to enter the enclosed area and not to shut down or interrupt G.A.L.E.N, which could be lethal to an unknown number of entities. Connection [COPYRIGHT] is to be disconnected.

G.A.L.E.N is named as the system that the gate and the Osrat device speak about. It is the only place the name appears in the sources, besides the Osrat notes (see 05-world-and-lore.md).

### 7.2 The Wasteland

The Wasteland lies outside the district. It has:

- **Ghost apartments**: a lost friend's flat, a burned apartment (its tenant says a man watched from outside), and a void beyond a door.
- **Shadow figures**: Jacob Cooper, the shed woman, Shadow Mike, the tunnel shadow woman, Father Tobias's shadow, the Lagerfeld shadows, and two fused blobs.
- **Mushroom creatures**: Tootti, Tonttu and Tuutti.
- **A field of red rocks** where a device is set up. The player is asked to collect red rocks there.
- **The red stones**: a black pendant's stone is warm to the touch, and the Osrat notes say the red stones may cause the pull to the other side.
- **The Other Side** lies behind it all (see 05-world-and-lore.md).

### 7.3 The prison hole

Colton lives in a hole in the Gatehouse wall, seen from a cell. He says a lockpick is under the bed and that the guard sleeps often.

## 8. Outside areas and other places

- **Black Swamp**: the source of the canal that became the Open Sewer. A church is said to be in the swamp, reached through the Bazaar, where Ines's cult contacts can be found.
- **Turnip hill**: Mr. Duck's hometown, where the fungal disease is said to have begun, when farmers found green mushrooms in an abandoned building.
- **The school courtyard**: a burial ground was built in the courtyard of a school because there was no other space (Krista's account).
- **Whiprock island**: planned quarantine zone (2017 article).
- **Uhlu**: a neighbouring area named in the quarantine warning.
- **Orefell and Cellecter**: towns on the 1990s railway line; Orefell is the nearest Stalburg Bank branch named.
- **Stalburg Central**: a city district that needs a permit, an ID pass, and a negative test to enter.
- **Stalburg Steel**: a steelworks that collapsed in 1981 (also a factory the Bazaar's Tor Bergstahl's father worked at).
- **SNPP** (Stalburg Nuclear Power Plant): on a small lake downstream of the Two Gorges Dam (per Anatoli).
- **Walter Co.**: the building company that built the Obenseuer tenements.
- **Merkki Street shop** (MuddyMarkku's cigar advert) and **Obenseuer Road 2** (addresses for subscriptions and invoices).
- **Italy and Poland**: named only in item texts (wine and pierogi).
- **Stolland**: the national government's country.
- **Hammer Valley**: a far place, where an old tunnel accident is recalled and where another tunnel B2 exists.

## 9. Factions, organisations and authorities

### 9.1 Government and the state

- **Stolland's government** (the national state): plans to eradicate green mushrooms, placed diagnosed people in a quarantine zone inside Obenseuer, refuses to expand the district, and stopped issuing new passports. Karl Hagen negotiated with it so tourists could still come, in exchange for strict controls on green mushroom use.
- **The Stalburg authorities**: the city's administration. They control the central district (which needs a permit) and the bus relocations. Some families are allowed to live outside the slum gates.
- **Stalburg Times** (newspaper): articles in 2014 (Obenseuer history, the Underground City legend) and 2017 (the quarantine zone). It reported 72 new cases in one weekend.
- **Mayor Jean Wallin** (2017) asked people to avoid Obenseuer and Uhlu.
- **Municipality of Obenseuer**: merged into the City of Stalburg in 1973, per the notes.

### 9.2 Police, emergency and health

- **The police** (Firplas police station, named in the legend, has cells): patrol the streets, take reports, arrest for crimes, and dismiss the player's reports when they involve a powerful figure. They are slow and often absent. Their word on the Passmore report is that they will not get involved further.
- **Emergency services (112)**: take calls, ask for location and landmarks, send officers and fungal treatment units.
- **The Fungal Disease Treatment Unit (FDTU)**: decontaminates buildings (Hotel Korso, a rat outbreak), collects the dead and infected, and runs "host disposal" with five-metre distance rules. Decontamination costs about 600 RM and takes one to four days. Containment dispatches were suspended at the time of one call.
- **Dr. Pena's clinic** (Deekula): doctor with a turn system, prescriptions go to the Tenement pharmacy.
- **The Tenement pharmacy** (Mona Hall, Patrik Axelsson): dispenses prescriptions only with a doctor's note.
- **The Obenseuer Skeida pharmacy**: named by Pate and in the items text (pharmacy keycard).

### 9.3 The Bazaar

- **Bazaar administration** (N-O Company): registers people, reviews each person, and runs elevator and mine access.
- **Bazaar Chamber of Commerce**: a board of business interests. It wants growth, fears central government, and wants the player's tenement absorbed. Its shop cannot open until the facade and courtyard are upgraded.
- **Karl Hagen** (the King of the Bazaar): leads the Bazaar. Interested in fungal disease treatments.
- **Bazaar security**: keeps troublemakers in the Fungi Boutique area, controls doors, and runs the casino's guards.
- **Bazaar jail**: staff, lockers, cells.
- **The Bazaar Chamber's board** works with the Bazaar's leader.

### 9.4 Banks, money and finance

- **Stalburg Bank**: a national bank. Customer service by phone, branches in Orefell, debit cards, netbank, loans. It refuses card requests by phone and wants paper forms.
- **Bank of Bazaar**: not an official bank. Its vault was robbed by Heikki Mailanen.
- **OS Power Company**: a power utility with shares sold on the stock exchange. Its reception is in the Mallment.
- **Samuel Godman's Legal Remedies**: sells legal action to clear crimes with the police or militia, and to unfreeze bank accounts. Invoices go to the player's address.
- **Land and Property Register**: a central district office for notarised deeds.

### 9.5 Moonshiners and drink

- **Moonshining Cooperative (Pontikka)**: run by Arvo Kirkastaja. Courses, exam, membership and recommendations. Membership unlocks equipment and a greenhouse. Many bar owners belong to it.
- **Osmo Olut**: the brewery, the biggest employer in the district. Its founder is Osmo Saarinen, a municipal councillor. It agreed to a mining deal on condition the old tunnels are maintained.
- **Stollandia Vodka**, **Socca Junior** and other brands: described in 05-world-and-lore.md.

### 9.6 Gangs and criminal groups

- **Devil's Angels**: Malone's old gang. Its boss died, Malone led, Harold Rimas took over, and the group grew orange and then green mushrooms. Harold and some members left Stalburg. Seamus and Malone are former members.
- **Svamppatrullen** ("mushroom patrol"): a gang that deals and smuggles green mushrooms. Its base is on the top floor of Deekula C. Its boss is in jail. Its men have thrown a body sack into the canal (per Finlay).
- **Sköldgangster**: Ville's gang. Named in Julius's door lines.
- **The Quarantined**: a young gang that talks about "what they deserved". It is new.
- **Julius Öberg's door**: a gatekeeper for "the boss" (a penthouse figure). Claiming to be sent by Ville gets the player through.
- **The boss**: an unseen figure with a penthouse and a bank room, who is on vacation or in prison.
- **Ivar's vending machine**: a small underground trade in shrooms and orange powder.
- **Bazaar team**: a group that broke into the western tunnels.
- **Rob's ex-apprentice**: a dead man, Robin.
- **Stalburg Underground**: see section 9.7 below.

### 9.7 Cults, churches and movements

- **Stalburg Underground**: the group that "showed" Ines the truth, gave her the drink and sent her to the gate. Ines calls it more than a cult. Its goals are unclear. Its members may be reached through a church in the swamp. A neighbour named in Father Tobias's letter is said to belong to the group and to have promised help to a sick man.
- **Redemption**: an aid church in the Bazaar that gives pea soup to people who pray. Run by Fidan Tanner. Volunteers work six-hour shifts. Donors gain access to the Bazaar door.
- **Welho circle and guild of alchemists**: Welhö calls himself a member of the alchemists' guild. The Welho tradition includes the Suurwelho (Erto), the Welho's Mark and the Sacred Seal of Welhous. Apprentices (Mikael) and the co-op (Arvo) are linked.
- **Greenwar**: an environmental organisation. Ville's former boss led its hippie branch.
- **Tunnel people / the underground**: a group behind the tunnel door. They refuse entry without the right password.
- **The Sauna Club**: an all-male club that meets in a mine space.
- **Church in the swamp**: where Ines's cult contacts may be found.

### 9.8 Companies and services

- **Rob-in Build and Repair** (Rob Madsen): renovations, toilets, flood doors, coffee makers. In the slums.
- **Urho Siltanen's engineering**: electrics, plumbing, heating for tenements.
- **Samuel Jonasson's contract work**: renovations in Deekula.
- **OS Mining Services** (Toivo): machinery and tunnel supports.
- **Open Homes Company** (Anni Kiinteistö): luxury house in the Bazaar, 220 000 RM, with strict paperwork for selling apartments.
- **Fungispring LTD**: mushroom-growing cabinets for 12 500 RM each, business customers only, a month of delivery.
- **Swamp Publishing LLC**: publisher of the Open Sewer magazine and other titles. Its telemarketing office is in Kolhola. Subscriptions need an address; the player can cancel by phone.
- **Hobbyprof publishing**: subscriptions to Puuhari (24 RM monthly) and ARG (12 RM monthly).
- **Stalburg Environment Services**: former employer of Ivan Kapustanov.
- **Stalburg University of Technology**: accepted Veeti. Its student housing association is in the sources.
- **Sossu**: a long apartment queue, probably a welfare office. Unclear.
- **Walter Co.**: built the tenements.
- **Stalburg Steel**: a steelworks that collapsed in 1981.
- **SOMC**: a mining company that left a shaft and a large vein under the Bazaar industrial area.
- **S.N.W.**: a state programme (one source expands it as Stolland Nuclear Weapons; the expansion is not confirmed elsewhere) that moved nuclear material through the old metro tunnels, discontinued after the 1986 accident. Named in Heska's speculation about the repository's waste, and in a letter about an old map.

- **Einar's crew**: a group of miners (Veikko, Kalle, Olavi, Einar) with their own surface apartment.
- **Joran's hotel**: a hotel inside the mines.

## 10. How the factions relate

The sources describe these relations, roughly:

- **The government vs the Bazaar.** The government refuses to expand Obenseuer and stopped issuing passports; the Bazaar is angry about deliveries and uses outsiders in its quarrels. The Chamber fears central government.
- **The Bazaar's internal politics.** The Chamber wants the slums moved to the swamp and the Bazaar expanded. Karl Hagen leads the N-O Company and works with the Chamber. The Bazaar's security works with the casino.
- **The Bazaar vs the mines.** The Bazaar controls the surface elevator and the permits; Management in the mines collects the tax and issues the permit.
- **The gangs.** Svamppatrullen and Sköldgangster fight over the top floor of Deekula C and the green-mushroom trade. The Devil's Angels are a dead or dying gang. The Quarantined are a new, violent group.
- **Church and co-op.** Redemption competes for the Bazaar's support through donations. The co-op has its own courses and its own membership requirements, and it is linked to Welhö and Erto.
- **Stalburg Underground and the cults.** The underground group works through Ines and reaches its members through the swamp church. Its relation to Stalburg, Redemption and the mines is unclear.
- **The police.** They are weak, distant, and sometimes bought or bribed. They work with the FDTU on bodies.
- **Osmo Olut.** The brewery is the biggest employer, has a deal with the mines, and has its bottles recur as currency, bribes and favours in the Murju and Welho threads.
- **The Stalburg Bank and the Bank of Bazaar.** The Bank of Bazaar is a separate, unofficial bank, robbed by Heikki Mailanen with the manager's help.
