# Obenseuer: Characters

Scope: every named character the digests and the raw files name, grouped by place or role, with role, home or work, personality, relationships, what they want and what happens to them in the quest lines. Conversation ids are given as [id] (or [id:entry]) for source. Empty stubs and unused actors are listed at the end. Story-level context is in 01-overview.md; quests in 04-questlines.md; factions and places in 03-places-and-factions.md.
Status: unverified source (digested by a model from game text, not played)
Related: the other lore documents
**SPOILERS: this document tells the whole story of the game.**

Note on names: names are kept as written. Some names collide across files (Mike, Jacob Cooper, Pera or Pena, Mr. Door Phone, Welhö and Suurwelho). These are listed in 06-open-questions.md.

## 1. The player's own building and its residents

The sources treat the player's tenement as one building, though the texts never name it (the one by the canal, recognised by Lasse [323]; the one over the unstable mine tunnel [224]; and the one where Malone and the Crazy Neighbor live). Residents come and go.

**Malone Petronis** [4], [209], [210], [211], [67], [226], [239]
- Role: former leader of the Devil's Angels, ex-salesman of orange mushroom powder, a chronic coughing smoker. In the first meeting he calls the player "turnip boy" and insists he has a right to stay.
- Home: an apartment in the building the player now owns. Later he is very ill, and his meeting with Seamus takes place in his own flat (per [210]).
- Personality: bitter, theatrical, funny, self-pitying. Asks for eight glass bottles of Osmo Olut before he will talk about his past [4:116].
- Relationships: old friend Seamus Liepa (wants his body found, or says he is dead); Harold Rimas (took over the gang and left Stalburg with some members); Pentti Penttilä and Max Masher (fellow co-op members, per the sources).
- Wants: a renovated apartment for free, and his old gang's story told. Later, a reconciliation with Seamus.
- Quest lines: he gives the renovation chain, the Seamus body task, the orange mushroom lantern blueprint, the co-op recommendation for a pack of Norts, and janitor tasks once his apartment reaches renovation levels 3 and 4. A dark monologue in [226] has him in darkness asking for lights and apologising for his past. In [210] he speaks as if dying. Whether he dies is unclear (06-open-questions).

**Crazy Neighbor** [156], [164], [181], [198], [212], [251], [255], [256], [257], [348], [409], [410], [432]-[439]
- Role: a paranoid, comic neighbour living in one of the player's apartments. He is the most developed recurring NPC in the files.
- Personality: possessive, mocking, obsessed with visits. He addresses the player as his neighbour in nearly every line. He cleans and fluffs his pillows for "special" visits.
- Relationships: the player (who owes him, he says, an unpayable debt); Casper Colikke (whose casino he takes over and whom he hires as a substitute manager); Pia (he says he recovered her head and made a lantern from it [198]).
- Wants: visits three times a week, an upgrade (a balcony), rent he calls a reasonable contribution, which is always a surprise.
- Quest lines: visit and upgrade thread; he can arrange recommendation letters for the Moonshining Co-op [164:106-117]; he can deal with the casino owner if the apartment has an upgrade; he teleports the player if they trespass; he offers a multiple-choice list of who killed Pia and mocks each answer. Conversation [257] suggests he thinks the player is overdue for some fun; the meaning is unclear.

**Pate Rantanen** [2]
- Role: resident living in a cottage damaged in a flood. Says a pink turnip in his head controls him and must get "turnip moon" (moonshine).
- Wants: a mash tun and fermenting barrel, then turnip moonshine. He rewards the player with a moon item and warns that turnips make people feel pain.
- Relationships: a doctor's dog that saved him from water, and a "good doctor" the turnip likes (linked to the Skeida pharmacy [2:53-2:55]).
- He can be offered apartment 26.

**Mr. Duck** [7], [93], [616]-[622], [420]-[422], [418]
- Role: a man who claims to be the real Mr. Duck, obsessed with rubber ducks, who speaks in quack language. He is in the Speakeasy in the Tenement in one source [93] and hides in the building in another [7].
- Personality: manic and threatening when refused, cheerful when given what he wants. He once butchered ducks with an axe.
- Relationships: a rival "Mr. Duck" in a blue suit, and a blue rubber duck that he calls addictive and cursed. He says the fungal disease began at Turnip hill (his hometown).
- Wants: five live rubber ducks, an axe, and his lost blue duck back. He gives keys to the upstairs and money (200 RM) for the duck.
- Quest lines: [7] duck and axe requests; [93] the blue duck quest, which may end with a broken duck head and a new duck given anyway.

**Pia** [8], [163], [197]-[199], [102], [88]
- Role: a young woman with the fungal infection, living in a makeshift two-story house with a balcony beside Deekula C. Fredrik is her doctor and supplies her medicine.
- Personality: suspicious, sharp, dependent on Alice. Her eyes glow green. She believes the fungus has become a symbiosis.
- Relationships: Alice (her friend and carer), Fredrik (doctor), Joel Hietala (Alice's partner; she suspects him).
- Wants: her medicine, Alice back, and not to be blamed. Her relationship points rise and fall with the player's answers.
- Fate: the sources disagree. She is dead when the player arrives in several branches; the head is taken; the body is found with the head missing [197]. Another branch has Pia dying after the player gives her red extract (Fredrik's experiment) [163]. A third has her found dead in a sauna [222]. One source raises the question of whether Pia and Ines Hietala are the same person (see 06-open-questions).

**Alice** [8], [163], [184]-[189], [196], [664]-[688]
- Role: Pia's friend and carer, who brings Pia medicine. Missing for days.
- Relationships: Pia (close), Joel Hietala (her boyfriend in the tasks; described as his girlfriend in Ines's account). Seen with a man going into Tenement A (per Axel).
- Fate: most sources say she is dead. One body in a storage building has a ritual setup and holds a photo of Pia (probably her). Ines says she killed Alice. Pia says Alice was pregnant (the player's claim [8:171]).

**Joel Hietala** [8], [135], [184], [186], [200], [163], [242]
- Role: lives on the second floor of Tenement A. Alice's boyfriend (per tasks). Seen as Alice's partner by Johannes Eskola.
- Personality: anxious, says he cannot leave yet. He took the remote control from neighbour Johannes Eskola.
- Relationships: Alice (partner), Pia (suspicious of him), Ines (in one line Ines says she heard Alice arguing with Joel).
- Wants: to stop a woman he is worried about and to return to a body (in [200], unexplained).
- Fate: unclear. His state in [200] suggests he is dead or half-dead and still speaking. The sources do not settle it.

**Olle Leino** [27]
- Role: a new arrival and former army building worker. Says the housing crisis reaches even people in the police force.
- Relationships: an infected person he tried to protect. Apartment 64 can be offered to him.

**Oscar Nilsson** [15]
- Role: a father who came to Obenseuer with his son and wants a three-room flat with kitchen and bathroom.
- Relationships: Pete Coskelo, a fraudster who took more than 1000 RM from him with a promised apartment through an "Open Sewer gang" link.
- Wants: his flat, which he can pay for at 10 to 30 RM a day.

**Aleksi Kivi** [37]
- Role: an author writing "Seven trees". Someone stole his book and all his things from his shack.
- Beliefs: he thinks the shrooms gave him inspiration and took him from his shack into the Open Sewer. He has headaches.
- Quest lines: apartment 35 can be offered; after renting he starts a new book.

**Martta Lapinjoki** [11]
- Role: a waiting resident, apartment 39. Complains that her husband does nothing. Thanks the player after a rental.
- Relative: Jaska Virtanen [144] (needs to talk to his wife Martta; says he is useless).

**Pera Harju** [12]
- Role: a drunk resident who wants Osmo Olut or a mushroom rollie. After a rollie he becomes grateful and can be offered apartment 36.

**Nina Lund** [10]: a resident offered apartment 38. Little else. (Linked to David Lund in the sources.)

**Jarmo Pettinen** [59]: a resident who can be offered apartment 40. Relatives Ilona and Aino Pettinen [479], [480] are silent.

**Daniel Nilsson** [60] (and as a relative): says he may not talk to strangers.

**Sara Claine** [22]: a passenger from the quarantine bus who wanted a quarantine area. Apartment 65 can be offered.

**Milla Kolleen** [147]: a resident robbed while sleeping rough, who felt sick after microwaved watermelon. Says it is safe where she is.

**Johan Wider** [34]: a shack resident who warns against visitors and says he can taste rat meat.

**Stein Bergland** [38]: a resident who says people die every day and complains that Krista Annina has done nothing about the smell.

**Agnes Takala** [70]: a resident who needs a new antidepressant prescription from Dr. Pena. Can be offered a rental (apartment 59). Suggests the One Stop Shop robot sells all kinds of things.
- Husband **Ulrik Takala** [107]: sleeps on the street, does not feel safe even with police patrols. Says the pea soup next to Kolhola is tasty.

**Leon Borg** [74] and wife **Leena Borg** [74], [477]: Leon is a new security guard with no home. He gets an apartment (ResidentRented 63). Leena avoids Dr. Pena's office and has a husband, Leo, who sleeps near Tenement B.

**Hall Kääbus** [136], [252], [253] and **Kirka Kääbus** [137]: a married couple. Hall only grunts, because, in Kirka's words, alcohol and mushrooms wrecked his brain. Kirka can rent an apartment (94).

**Taneli Kurju** [138]: needs a roof. Could rent an apartment (91). Likes a beer and sugar flake mix.

**Fenella Hunt** [139] and **Moray Larson** [140]: telemarketers for the OS magazine. Fenella lives in the pod hotel while she waits months for an apartment in the "Sossu" queue (rentable, flag 95). Moray is glad not to be shouted at by customers.

**Per Otter** [401] and **Anu Otter** [402]: Per (rented flat 254) complains about midnight noise and his pension. Anu makes fish stew and hates otter jokes.

**Veeti Pärisevä** [452]: a student accepted into the Stalburg University of Technology. Needs a cheap flat (271). Drinks OS energy drink.

**Elsa Varis** [295]: plays INFRA. Says she pays rent on time. Will rent apartment 164 if available.

**Jaska Virtanen** [144]: see Martta above.

**Ivan Kapustanov** is in the Bazaar group (see section 4).

**Milla, Johan, Stein, Daniel and Jarmo** have no further role in the story.

## 2. The Center doorphone building [66]

A doorphone building with five floors and four or five named tenants per floor. The building is not named in the sources. Its names are the doorphone's options:

- Floor 1: Berg, Hietala, Abrahamsson, Halko. Answering the Halko door brings a complaint about beer.
- Floor 2: Eskola, Walker, Adams, Huang.
- Floor 3: Samuelsson, Falk, Moilanen, Ilves.
- Floor 4: Skuja, Jansons, Rummo, Putilin.
- Floor 5: Grachyov, Kettunen, Taylor, Ylitalo.

One resident says they have stopped using mushrooms, feel better than ever, and refuse any mushroom sale [66:129]. Most doors just buzz or swear. The Hietala and Eskola names match the tenants of Tenement A (see section 3), so the building may be a second Tenement A building (unclear).

## 3. Deekula, Kolhola, Tenement A and Tenement B

### Deekula (Deekula A, B, C)

**Max Masher** [14], [32], [56], [63], [81], [112]
- Role: a mash maker in Deekula B, apartment B10 on the top floor. Speaks in short, repeated, fragmented phrases about mash. His head was hit with a hammer during a mushroom mash accident, which gave him his name.
- Relationships: Pentti Penttilä (his customer, who has not heard from him for a long time); Arvo Kirkastaja (the co-op leader he phones to recommend the player); Doctor "Pera" or Pena (who might fix his mash).
- Wants: help with mash, and punishment for the door that hurt him. The player may kick the "evil door" (objective Punish44).
- Quest lines: the "Me mash!" chain. The player fetches sugar and yeast; Max is paid a small amount and haggled up; he calls Arvo, whose reaction depends on the player's answers.

**Adam Holmgren** [81]
- Role: a Deekula tenant who moved into an empty flat whose former residents are gone for good. An Osmo Olut collector who sells an Osmo Minitrain miniature for 2500 OC.
- Personality: says everyone here is sick and deluded. Calls Osmo Saarinen a legend.
- Information: says Max lives in B10.

**Keijo Korsonen** [39]: a retired Deekula man who mistakes the player for "Mark", a former drinking partner at the Osmo Hill bar. Watches text TV and Iskelmä TV. Lonely.

**Amanda Korhonen** [63], [65], [634], [636], [638]
- Role: a Deekula B3 tenant evicted after a quarrel with her neighbour, a painter who collects radio receivers. She says the neighbour killed Tea (the cat). She has a storage key for storage B4.
- Relationships: the neighbour, the woman in bed (under a blanket, answering only in broken fragments), and the body in the bathroom which the player names Jacob Cooper.
- Wants: the player to recover a device that lets her talk with the dead woman (she offers 200 RM).
- Quest lines: tell her about the bathroom body and the woman in bed, and report the body to the police. The police are slow to act.

**Jim Nilsen** [21]: a Deekula B "hacker" who catches the player in a locked building. Any answer leads to prison.

**The priest (Father Tobias)** [78], [79], [145], [316], [231]
- Role: a priest in Deekula B, flat B1. In one branch he is infected and near death, and he is said to have worked at a church in the swamp. He hid a passage behind a bookshelf.
- As a shadow [79]: says the player should not be here, is between two states, and is not the original source of what is happening. He says he is fading.
- Letters [12145180852312], [12145180852316]: a sick priest on leave who has heard of an other side and who says he must investigate before he speaks. Another letter, from a neighbour, thanks him for care given to her father; a neighbour in the Stalburg Underground promised help.
- Uncertainties: whether the B1 priest, the shadow Tobias and the priest who gave the pea soup recipe to Fidan are one man (see 06-open-questions).

**Ville Sköldgangster** [30], [148], [492]-[504]
- Role: a former tenant who stopped paying rent after the building's floor collapsed. Formerly a worker who failed a sabotage job for a leader of the environmental group Greenwar. He now offers the player sabotage work.
- Personality: gruff, boozy, unrepentant. Offers the player a job after drinks.
- Relationships: the Svamppatrullen gang (a rival on the top floor of Deekula C), and the boss in jail (his enemy).
- Quest lines: sabotage by a poisoned Socca Junior bottle. He pays the player (1280 money in one file).

**David Lund** [71], [111], [35]
- Role: built the bridge in Deekula, sells passage, and sells hand-made passports for 100 RM with no refund. Lives in apartment C6.
- Personality: hostile to complainers. On the toll line he tells callers to find another route to the other side if they will not pay [111:9].
- Relationships: a Stalburg unemployed resident who fears losing Sossu subsidies (probably the same person, unclear). Potato Bob is an alias the player may give him.
- Quest lines: the bridge purchase (his lifetime pass costs 2000 RM; the full bridge is refused; the phone line is a bridge toll service).

**Dr. Pena MD** [92], [89], [477], [70]
- Role: a doctor in Deekula with a turn-number system. Needs a turn ticket (item 13410) before he sees you. Writes prescriptions for the Tenement pharmacy.
- Relationships: Agnes Takala (who needs prescriptions), Mona Hall (the pharmacist), Leena Borg (who avoids him). Doctor "Pena" may be the same as "Pera" in Max's lines (unclear).

**Samuel Jonasson** [4], [35], [82]
- Role: a renovation and building-materials contractor in Deekula. Gives a free item on first contact. Explains renovation: pay the first installment, start with apartment walls, check the needs of future tenants, pay the final sum on completion. Offers a free workbench in the wooden extension.
- Personality: practical, businesslike. Finds the One Stop Shop clerk odd.

**Hank Möbelmann** [58], [108]
- Role: a furniture seller in Deekula C. Says he is in the wrong place for passports, but sells a passport route (about 100 RM, haggled down to 50 or 75). Gives the password [COPYRIGHT].

### Kolhola (Tenement/Kolhola)

**Eliana Passmore** [72], [77], [145], [178]
- Role: professional photographer with a photography shop in the Kolhola building. Forges passports. Takes the photo and the passport arrives by mail after a couple of days.
- Personality: smart, guarded, closed for business at first, with a stock "temporarily closed" line. Calls the other Passmore's schemes her own problem.
- Relationships: Hanss (a close friend; she helped him reach the Bazaar with a forged passport), Anya (she knows her and expected to hear from her), Passmore (her former partner, who still owes her, she says), the priest (she asks the player to check on him).
- Wants: the priest's debt collected; the photo and a passport route.
- Quest lines: she is the source of the basement password [COPYRIGHT], and of the priest task. Her passports are the core of the passport route.

**Passmore** (Tenement B) [72], [145], [431], [77]
- Role: a gatekeeper of Tenement B, behind the O-market, who sold passports and now won't say why. Has a private bridge from his apartment to the Bazaar, used to move stock past the border.
- Personality: evasive about his private life, scheming, bitter.
- Relationships: Heikki Mailanen (his friend, who robbed the vault and whom he says tricked him), Theo Tammi (the bank manager), Eliana (his former partner, who forged for him).
- Wants: the Bank of Bazaar's money. He asks the player to join a heist and to find Heikki.
- Quest lines: the passport tasks, the bank heist and the police report about him.
- Note: Eliana says she is not on good terms with a man called Passmore, and that the player knocked on the wrong door. Whether this is the same man is unclear.

**Meri Haka** [146]: an elderly woman in Kolhola. Forgetful; threatens to call the police if the player will not pay with a pack of Zebra cigarettes. Thanks the player with 10 money if they bring the pack.

**Julius Öberg** [148], [149], [143]
- Role: lives behind a "mushroom door" in Kolhola and sells mushrooms. Acts as gatekeeper for "the boss".
- Personality: sly and comic. Asks who Mark is when told a present is for Mark.
- Information: the boss has an upstairs flat the player cannot reach; the boss will be free soon; his mother makes excellent turnip soup; a Socca Junior present was left on the boss's table.

**Jörge Af Guldsköld** [153]: runs an office in Kolhola. Does not intervene when the player breaks in. Says the cops will come eventually and that he has good insurance. The file spells his name both as Guldskjöld and Guldsköld.

**Börje Nyberg** [150], **Liouba Bennett** [151], **Rauha Taisto** [152]: minor Kolhola residents. Börje hates his customers. Liouba is busy. Rauha gives a dough recipe and talks about "dark times".

**Sam Morton** [134], [154]: a Kolhola tenant who wants late pizza, since Aziz's deliveries have stopped. Plays Maahis Mättö and says Maahis Mättö Origins is terrible. Plays online with Jim, who lives in the Deekula building.

**Mona Hall** [89], **Patrik Axelsson** [90]: pharmacy staff in the Tenement pharmacy. Mona sells prescription drugs only with a doctor's prescription, and calls the guards if the player harasses her. Patrik says nothing of use.

### Tenement A

**Fredrik** [135], [162], [163], [188], [190], [192], [77], [404], [424]-[428]
- Role: a doctor and scientist, who runs an unofficial lab in his flat, Tenement A apartment A10. Studies green mushrooms and the fungal disease.
- Personality: candid and worried, an outsider doctor who doubts the official government response.
- Relationships: Pia (his patient; he sends medicine via Alice), Anya (asks him to test samples), Dr. Evensen (a colleague whose unorthodox method Fredrik doubts), the operating-room patient (his test subject).
- Wants: a cure, and to help one particular person, according to Anya.
- Quest lines: the red mushroom experiment, the red extract, the medicine route, the doorphone reveal for Tenement A.

**Louie Green** [183], [184], [404], [135]
- Role: runs a mushroom lantern factory in Tenement A. Hires the player for an eight-hour shift at 65 OC per hour.
- Relationships: his wife Ayla Green. He says Hietala used to work at the factory and quit days ago.
- Note: his flags control the Murju doorphone replies (per [404]), so his role there is unclear.

**Ayla Green** [185]: his wife. Strict but nice, she says. They want to move somewhere warm. She hates her job and does not know her neighbours.

**Johannes Eskola** [186], [187]: a neighbour in Tenement A. Says Jimi broke into his flat and vanished. Warns about the lantern factory's noise. Says Joel lives on the second floor. Got Joel's keys from the building manager, who accepts Stollandia vodka as payment.

**Emma-Maija Taylor** [196]: sells cats. Has only seen cats lately.

**Jonne Reis** [189]: calls himself Obenseuer's only elevator expert, installs elevators with Urho Siltanen. Likes kebab and reads the Obenseuer magazine.

**Dr. Evensen** [188]: Fredrik's colleague (implied). Relies on a book Fredrik cannot name.

**Axel Norberg** [20], [119]-[126] (gatehouse), [124], [664]: see section 4.

**Louie and Joel and the patient**: see the Tenement A entries above. The operating-room patient [163], [188] is unnamed.

## 4. The Speakeasy and the Kurahaara brothers

**Pentti Penttilä** [33], [56]
- Role: owner of a speakeasy in a Tenement building, with a secret distillery room behind a button on the bar shelf. A co-op member.
- Personality: a black-bearded businessman who threatens to make "fecal kilju" if Max cannot make mash.
- Relationships: Max Masher (supplier), Arvo (co-op), the player (who is told to find Max).
- Quest lines: the "Me mash!" chain, and the secret room.

**Mr. Door Phone** [95], [109]
- Role: the intercom at the speakeasy door. He refuses his name at first, then calls himself Mr. Door Phone. He lets in only someone who claims to be Malone's assistant, and says he has not heard from Malone for a long time.
- Note: Simppa Kurahaara in [109] calls himself "Mr. Doorphone" too, so the two may be the same (unclear).

**Simppa Kurahaara** [109]: works in the Speakeasy bar. Says the brothers have no interest in women. Has a headache.

**Jomppe Kurahaara** [87], [712]: a farmer who grows turnips and vegetables in his greenhouse with his brothers. The three were once garbagemen. Accepts only plants grown in the greenhouse and asks for special orders (20 cucumbers, 10 peppers, 30 potatoes). Speaks with a Finnish accent in English; a Finnish branch exists.

**Lönkka Kurahaara** [99], [142]: sells produce from a stall. Says he is not in the mood to talk and complains about his back.

## 5. The Bazaar: administration, gatehouse and the Chamber

**Axel Norberg** [20], [119]-[126], [664]
- Role: the Bazaar border inspector at the gatehouse. Checks passports and bags, sells the registration form for 100 RM, and can take a bribe. Refusing him sends the player to prison.
- Personality: officious, suspicious, and open to bribery. He saw Alice with a man going to Tenement A.
- Quest lines: forged passports pass his checks (with the registration fee).

**Karl Hagen** [77], [313], [331], [386], [456]
- Role: the leader of the Bazaar, called [COPYRIGHT]. Works in the N-O Company building, which is the Chamber of Commerce's headquarters.
- Personality: rarely speaks in the files; his daughter died of a fungal disease (her skin glowed) [313].
- Relationships: Anya sends the player to him. The Chamber's board serves him.
- Wants: interest in fungal disease treatments; the red mushroom extract is a bargaining chip.

**Burt Kraut** [13], [63]: Bazaar Chamber of Commerce. Says only "...". His name appears in the variables about the body report and about Tea Henderson's death, so he may know about the B3 body.

**Jussi Pöhinä** [453], [456]: Chamber chairman. Wants calm and a tidy tenement. Warns that the government would dislike expansion.

**Katriina Vermund** [331], [454]: the Chamber's receptionist, who opens the conference room only if a name is given. Vanja Bjorkman says she brews good coffee.

**Vanja Bjorkman** [456], [457]: Chamber member. Pushes to move the slums to the swamp, build a bridge checkpoint and a wall, and run canal tours after the fungal plague is wiped out. Suggested a new OC design, which the board refused.

**Svein Regnell** [458]: Chamber member who loves shiny things.

**Tor Bergstahl** [340], [352]: a Bazaar Industrial official. Gives the history of the building and sells the idea of mining. Says his father worked at Stalburg Steel. Issues elevator access after a registration check. Warns the player that the tenement is falling apart after Toivo speaks to him.

**Penni-Pena** [24], [338], [357]: a Mentti loan officer. Gives loans and takes payments. Limits loans based on the value of an apartment block. Also appears as a Bazaar resident name "Penni-Pena" [24], whose PocketBankHasAccount flag is linked to her (unclear).

**Samuel Godman** [299], [235]: "Legal Remedies". Sells clearing of crimes with police or the militia, and unfreezing bank accounts (about a week). Invoices go to the player's address. An unpaid invoice blocks further service.

**Paul Lauwens** [300]: a phone contact who deflects requests and asks for applications rather than walk-ins. Says people still have not forgotten a demolished historic building. Mentions a friend named Mark.

**Anni Kiinteistö** [461]: Open Homes Company realtor. Sells the luxury house for 220 000 RM. Explains that selling an apartment needs a notarised deed, blueprints and an energy certificate.

**Johanna Faust** [364]: OS Power reception desk.

**Justyna Krystiana** [368]: sells furniture only to registered businesses.

**Pertti Korhonen** [352]: maintenance firm man who smuggles people into the industrial zone for 150 RM, and into the quarantine zone in a van during the water plant's weekly downtime for 1000 RM.

**Warden (Bazaar Jail)** [346], [481], [482]
- Role: runs the jail. Sends the player to prison when they enter a restricted area. Inside, sells or gives items according to the player's stats (can opener, duck, medicine, bandages, mushrooms) and can set a sentence and take payment for release.

**Riina Pamppumäki** [347]: security lobby officer. Refuses pizza deliveries to inmates, even at a bribe; only a high bribery check changes her answer.

**Lätty** [449]: an inmate waiting for a pizza. Thanks the player and mentions a lawyer.

**Jane Miller** [403]: hires the player for a drone delivery into the quarantine area: 500 RM now, 2000 RM on delivery. Says the drone goes to a programmed drop point and returns, and can be disabled by the enemy.

**Anatoli Mustaruoho** [367] (Murju): former power plant operator. Tells the SNPP accident story over moonshine. Gives a graphite item as a reward.

**Mikkel Purola** is in the mines group (section 7).

## 6. The Bazaar: shops, services and the casino

**Rob Madsen** [35]: owner of Rob-in Build and Repair. Offers renovations, toilets, flood doors, coffee makers. His former apprentice Robin started a company, nearly went bankrupt, and then got the player as a customer. Rob says Robin always thought he could manage everything alone, and that he is dead. Points the player to the engineer Urho Siltanen and to a loan agent in Mentti B.
- Note: Rob-in in the slums per [288] (Urho points there).

**Rob Rosso** [254]: printer. Sells prints of landscapes and portraits, and custom images on flags.

**Urho Siltanen** [35], [288]: engineer for electrics, plumbing and heating in tenements.
