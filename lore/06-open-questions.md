Scope: the contradictions between the sources, the points the digests marked "unclear", and the facts that only one weak source supports. Each entry gives what each source says, with conversation or note ids, and whether the raw text was checked (raw checks were made with narrow searches in the raw sources). ## 1. Contradictions between sources

### 1.1 Two opening scenes
- The intake interviewer [83] hands the player a tenement block and asks personal questions. The player's package waits by a vending machine.
- The opening cutscene [415] has the player as the last of six people, digging red rocks with a pickaxe. Success revokes a pending relocation, and Welhö appears and starts a card game [416].
- Both are described in the digests as the start. The Start/Interviewer actor row and Welhö's row share an entry (the actors file lists Welhö as the Start interview's second speaker), so Welhö may be the interviewer under another name. Unclear. Whether [415] is the normal opening or a tutorial is also unclear (09_11 notes this).

### 1.2 Dates of the metro and the mine
- The first metro line opened in 1953 (answer to "1953?", raw [182:27-30]) and in 1962 (raw [COPYRIGHT]). The same character gives both. The player's quiz options were 1950, 1953, 1982 or I do not know. A digest says the tunnels were built in the 1970s (conversation [182]).
- The Obenseuer ore mine closed in 1958 (localisation notes, background piece). Another source [340] says that half the district was still active mining ground until operations halted [COPYRIGHT] and the area was rezoned. These cannot both be the closing date as written.
- The municipality merged into Stalburg in 1973 (notes). The area became a tenement district from the late 1970s (notes).

### 1.3 Mike, the Bergmann tunnels and Welhö's apprentice
- "Mushroom Mike" [75] is a former worker in the Bergmann tunnels, the father of Emilie Palmer, who grew mushrooms that grow on him. He is alive in [75] and gives the player mushrooms.
- "Mikael Arnaldur ('Mike')" [270, 400] is Welhö's former apprentice, who lives in a slum hut and was ordered to burn two books.
- A notice signed [COPYRIGHT] [12145180852296] and the tasks say [COPYRIGHT] [698] (raw task text). If Mike is dead, Mushroom Mike cannot be the one who gives mushrooms in [75].
- Shadow Mike [76] is the ghost of Mike, with the B2 key in his former body's hand.
- The digests treat all four as one character in some places and as separate people in others. The sources do not settle it.

### 1.4 Pia's fate and location
- [8] (digest of 01_02): Pia is alive, with a three-month infection and suspicions about Joel.
- [163] (digest of 05_06): Pia is dead or not at home if the player gave her the red extract. Fredrik says he expected it.
- [197]-[199]: Pia's body is in her room, with the head missing. The Crazy Neighbor says he recovered the head and made a lantern.
- Notes (localisation, tasks [684]): Pia was found dead in the sauna.
- Conversation [198] (Crazy Neighbor): he says the cell door was already open when he got there. The digests do not say whether Pia was in a cell at all.
- Police lines [102] say [COPYRIGHT] (PiaPolice88), and the storage next to Deekula C (TellPiaAlice46).
- The sources do not agree on where Pia died, how, or whether the player's extract killed her.

### 1.5 Joel Hietala: partner, brother, or alive?
- Joel is Alice's boyfriend in the tasks and in Tatyana's and Johannes's lines.
- The digest of 07_08 says Ines's brother is Joel. The raw conversation [242] has Ines say she killed his girlfriend (Alice) when the player asks about Joel. The digest's "brother" claim is not confirmed by any line I checked.
- Joel's own state: a speech from a body in Tenement A [200] ([COPYRIGHT], [COPYRIGHT]). The digest says the state is unexplained.
- Fredrik gives the surname Hietala for the flat he names in the stairwell conversation (raw [163:168]), and Pia is also linked to the Hietala name in the digests. A notes letter signed "Hietala" confesses that a person was killed in a rage and asks for prayers for the victim's shadow. The text does not say whether the writer is Joel, Ines or Pia.

### 1.6 Ines and Pia: the same person?
- The digest of 03_04 raises whether Pia and "Ines Hietala" (reported at Kauha's Claim as involved in multiple murders) are one person. The raw sources show Ines as a separate character (canteen worker, blonde, in the mine room). No raw line I checked makes Pia and Ines one person. Treat it as a question raised by one digest only.

### 1.7 Alice's death and the shed woman

- Shadow shed woman [91]: a murdered woman who had surgery while pregnant, cannot remember her name, and died in a shed. Her identity is not given.
- Alice is said to have been pregnant (a claim the player makes [8:171]). The shed woman's pregnancy may be the same story or another.
- The digests disagree on whether the shed woman and the storage body are Alice.

### 1.13 Erto and the Suurwelho
- Welhö says the Suurwelho lives at a tower [270:36]. Other NPCs describe Suurwelho as a lunatic in a hut (Elias [304]), a leader of an underground tenement cult (Ulla [315]), and as someone who wants the Welho title.

### 1.14 The currency mapping (OC and RM)
- 01_02: OC is "sewer coins" or "Open Sewer Coins". RM is "money" or "real money". Doctor [31] contrasts real money with OC.
- 03_04: OC is the intake's currency. RM is used for passports, fines and donations.
- 07_08: RM is used for most prices; OC is used for diesel and for a sewing shop's hourly pay. The digest says the meaning of OC is unclear.
- 09_11: RM is Stollandian Marks; OC is the Obenseuer currency. The digest adds that rent and loans are in RM and bets, bribes and kebabs are often in OC.
- Items (raw texts): "Open Sewer Coin" is a local coin; "Real Money" is a legal tender. This is the only text that describes the currencies.
- The mapping OC = Open Sewer Coin and RM = Real Money is an inference (see 05-world-and-lore.md, section 6).
- Pertti's quarantine trip costs 1000 RM in the text, but its script removes 100 RM (09_11). Possibly a bug.

## 5. Summary

The sources are rich in named people and branching quests, but they disagree on a handful of core points. The most important open questions are: which intro is canonical; whether the mines, the metro and the mine's closing date fit together; whether Mike, Mikael and Mushroom Mike are one man; how Pia, Alice, Joel and Ines fit in the killings; whether Malone and Seamus are alive; who the Suurwelho is; what the currencies are; what the code [COPYRIGHT] means; and what the Other Side is. The digests are most solid on the tenement, the Bazaar's passports and bank, and the economy. They are weakest on the Other Side, the Osrat device, the Welho lore and the endings.
